# KO-PUSAKA Asset360 — 10-minute demonstration script

A suggested run-through for showing the platform to management. Every figure quoted below is
computed live from the seeded dataset, so the numbers on screen will match.

## 1. The public experience (2 minutes)

1. Open **`/`**. Point at the hero: *Turn Every Property Into an Opportunity.*
2. Use the search bar — pick **Kuching** and **Shoplot** — and press Search.
3. On `/properties`, switch between **grid**, **list** and **map** views. Filter by price.
4. Open **Saradise Corner Retail Lot**. Walk through the gallery, price, specifications and
   the enquiry form. Note the *Verified Property* badge, property ID and last-updated date.
5. Submit an enquiry with any name and phone number. Note the reference number returned.
6. Resize to a phone width to show the sticky **Enquire Now** and **WhatsApp** bar.

## 2. The enquiry becomes a lead (1 minute)

1. Follow the *See it appear in the CRM* link, or go to **`/portal/leads`**.
2. The enquiry you just submitted is at the top of the **New Lead** column, already assigned
   to the officer responsible for that property, with a follow-up set for today.
3. Drag it to **Contacted**, then open it and add a note.

## 3. The executive view (3 minutes)

1. Go to **`/portal`**.
2. Read the top row: total properties, occupancy rate, monthly rental income and — in black —
   **unrealised monthly income**. That last figure is the commercial argument for the platform.
3. Point at **Action required**: vacancies beyond 90 days, unrealised income, overdue
   follow-ups, rental arrears. Click any alert to land on the records behind it.
4. Show the portfolio donut, income trend, vacancy ranking, pipeline funnel and referral
   performance. Click a KPI card to open its filtered list.

## 4. Idle asset activation (1 minute)

1. Open **`/portal/idle-assets`**.
2. The watchlist is ranked by days vacant, with potential monthly and annual rental beside
   each property, plus enquiries and last marketing activity.
3. Press **Mark marketed** on a property to record activity — the health score updates.

## 5. Income and tenancy risk (1 minute)

1. **`/portal/rental`** — expected against collected, collection rate, arrears by property.
   Press **Mark received** on an outstanding record and watch the collection rate move.
2. **`/portal/tenancies`** — expiries at 90, 60 and 30 days, with renewal position.

## 6. The referral engine (2 minutes)

1. **`/portal/referrers`** — approve the pending applicant, then open a referrer record to
   show clicks, enquiries, viewings and deals, with the link and downloadable QR code.
2. Open **`/referrer`** to show the partner's own view.
3. Copy a property share link with a referral code, open it in a new tab, and submit an
   enquiry — the lead appears in the CRM already attributed to that referrer.

## 7. Adding to the portfolio (optional, 1 minute)

1. **`/portal/properties` → Add property**. Enter a name, address, area, rent and size, leave
   *Publish immediately* ticked and create it.
2. You land on its scorecard. Open **`/properties`** on the public site and search for it —
   it is listed, with its own page, share links and QR code.
3. On **`/portal/leads` → Log enquiry**, record a walk-in against any property and attribute it
   to a referrer.

## 8. Reports and governance (1 minute)

1. **`/portal/reports`** — export any of the nine management reports as CSV.
2. **`/portal/admin`** — roles and permissions, referral settings (no commission rate is
   hard-coded), taxonomies, and the Supabase integration path.
3. Switch persona in the top-right to **Mohd Ridzuan (Finance Executive)** — the sidebar
   narrows to the dashboard, rental and reports. Switch back to see full access return.

> Use **Reset demo data** in the portal sidebar to restore the dataset before the next
> demonstration.
